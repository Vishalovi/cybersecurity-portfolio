# Botium Toys – Risk Assessment

## 1. Assessment Overview

This risk assessment identifies potential threats and vulnerabilities affecting the fictional Botium Toys organisation.

The assessment considers the confidentiality, integrity, and availability (CIA) of business information and technology assets.

**Initial Risk Rating:** High (8/10), based on the case study's initial assessment.

## 2. Key Assets Identified

| Asset | Business Importance |
|---|---|
| Customer personal information | Protects customer privacy |
| Payment card information | Prevents financial fraud and data exposure |
| Employee devices | Provides access to business systems |
| Internal network | Supports business connectivity |
| Business applications | Supports daily operations |
| Customer and transaction databases | Maintains business records |
| Physical equipment | Supports IT and business operations |
| Legacy systems | Supports existing business processes |

## 3. Risk Register

| ID | Risk Description | Potential Business Impact | Initial Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | Excessive employee access to sensitive information | Unauthorised access and data exposure | High |
| R02 | Lack of segregation of duties | Fraud, unauthorised changes and control failures | High |
| R03 | Payment card information is not encrypted | Exposure of sensitive financial information | Critical |
| R04 | Weak password requirements | Account compromise and unauthorised access | High |
| R05 | No centralised password management | Inconsistent credential protection | High |
| R06 | No intrusion detection system | Delayed identification of suspicious activity | High |
| R07 | No regular backup of critical data | Data loss and prolonged business disruption | Critical |
| R08 | No documented disaster recovery plan | Extended recovery time after a major incident | High |
| R09 | Legacy systems lack scheduled maintenance | Unpatched vulnerabilities and system instability | High |
| R10 | Security monitoring and detection gaps | Delayed incident response | High |

## 4. Existing Security Measures

The case study identifies the following existing safeguards:

- Firewall rules restrict network traffic.
- Antivirus software is installed and monitored.
- CCTV cameras are available at the premises.
- Physical locks protect facilities and equipment.
- Fire detection and prevention arrangements are available.
- Data availability and integrity controls are reported to be in place.
- Privacy policies and breach notification procedures have been developed.

These safeguards provide some protection but do not eliminate the identified risks.

## 5. Risk Prioritisation

### Critical Risks

- Unencrypted payment card information.
- Absence of regular backups for critical data.

### High Risks

- Excessive access privileges.
- Lack of segregation of duties.
- Weak password controls.
- No centralised password management.
- Absence of intrusion detection.
- No documented disaster recovery plan.
- Inadequate legacy system maintenance.

## 6. Recommended Risk Treatment

| Risk Area | Recommended Action |
|---|---|
| User access | Implement role-based access control and least privilege |
| Segregation of duties | Define conflicting roles and implement independent approvals |
| Payment data | Apply appropriate encryption and key management |
| Password security | Establish strong password and authentication requirements |
| Password management | Implement centralised credential management |
| Intrusion detection | Introduce IDS/IPS and security monitoring |
| Backups | Establish scheduled backups and restoration testing |
| Disaster recovery | Document and test disaster recovery procedures |
| Legacy systems | Introduce scheduled patching and vulnerability reviews |
| Monitoring | Establish security logging, alerting and incident escalation |

## 7. Audit Conclusion

The initial assessment indicates a high-risk security environment, particularly in access management, payment data protection, backup and recovery, and security monitoring.

Management should prioritise critical risks, assign control owners, establish remediation deadlines, and monitor corrective actions through periodic reviews.

**Note:** Risk priorities are preliminary qualitative assessments for this educational case study. They should be validated through detailed likelihood and impact analysis.
